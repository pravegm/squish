# Marketing takes (ci/marketing-record.sh; docs/marketing/ASSETS.md). One recording per line, read by the recorder for
# its DEVICE and GROUP (the workflow's jobs), cut into clips/<name>.mov for ci/marketing/compose.mjs.
#
# device|group|name|scenario|stop|clip from|clip to|takes
#  - device: p69 (iPhone 6.9" simulator, the P-48 touch press) or duo (the iPhone Duo simulator; the press is the app's
#    own `hingescript`, fed through its hinge input).
#  - group: which recording job takes it (marketing.yml runs groups in parallel; each group pays its own boot/warm-up).
#  - scenario: the -squishDemo keys (Squish/App/DemoMode.swift). `@P69` / `@DUO` expand to the base keys below.
#  - stop: when to stop recording, "<mark>+<seconds>" (any marks file: preview-marks, preview-script, slomo-marks,
#    shelffill-marks), or "<mark>+<s>,<mark>+<s>" alternatives. The recorder gives up after 180 s.
#  - clip from / clip to: what goes into the clip, as preview-lib.sh spec_epoch specs ("<mark>±<s>", comma fallbacks),
#    or "end" (the end of the recording). Marks are re-based to clip seconds in clips/<name>.marks.txt.
#  - takes: up to this many recordings; the first whose longest frame gap (render stall) in the action is <= 0.25 s
#    wins, else the smallest gap.
#
