# Squish

A hydraulic press in your pocket. A paid toy for iPhone Duo: place an object on the inner display, slowly close the phone and watch it crush in real time, then reopen to collect the flattened result.

## Layout

- `research/` — design research reports (haptics, sound, visuals, psychology/UX, hinge UX, market)
- `design/` — the Squish design bible and specs
