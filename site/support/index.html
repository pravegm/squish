Quick answers

How do I press?

On iPhone Duo: open the phone fully on the inner screen, ideally lying flat on a table. Push the top half down slowly and the press follows your hand. Stop and it stops; open a little and it eases off. Close it fully for the ka-chunk, then open it again to see your specimen.

On any other iPhone: drag down on the screen and the P-48 press follows your finger. Lift your finger to let go.

With VoiceOver (when pressing by dragging): the press is one adjustable control. Swipe up with one finger to press down and swipe down to ease off.

Can I play on iPhone Duo without folding?

Yes. Open the Shelf, tap the cog to open Settings, and turn on Press without folding (under Feel). Then you drag down on the screen instead, which is handy one-handed or when folding isn't comfortable. Every object, unlock and achievement works this way.

Does Squish work on iPhones that don't fold?

Yes. Squish runs on any iPhone with iOS 18 or later. Phones that don't fold get the P-48, a drag-down bench press with the same engine, the same ten objects, the same sounds and haptics, and the same shelf and achievements.

On iPhone Duo, the two halves of the inner screen become the press plates and the hinge drives the press. After a press, the cover display shows the sealed press until you open the phone again.

Too loud, too crunchy, or too much buzzing?

Open the Shelf, tap the cog, and adjust:

  • Sound: turn it off entirely, or choose a Sound style. Workshop is the full mix; Soft is gentler, with rounder highs and fewer crunches.
  • Haptics: Off, Soft or Full.
  • Captions: big comic words like POP! and CRACK! for each moment, useful with the sound off.

Changes save as you go.

No sound?

Squish respects your iPhone's silent mode and mixes with any music you're playing. Turn silent mode off or turn the volume up, and check that Sound is on in Squish's Settings. Haptics are set separately, under Feel.

Does Squish support Reduce Motion?

Yes. Squish follows your iPhone's setting (Settings › Accessibility › Motion › Reduce Motion); its own Settings screen shows whether it's on. With Reduce Motion on, fragments slump and settle instead of flying, and interface animations are toned down.

How does Game Center work in Squish?

Game Center is optional. If you're signed in, achievements are reported as you earn them. If you're not, the achievements you earn are kept on your iPhone and sent once you sign in. To sign in, go to Settings › Game Center on your iPhone. Squish's Settings screen shows your Game Center status.

How do I unlock the other objects?

Just keep pressing. Six objects are open from the start, three more unlock through normal play (no purchases, ever), and one is a secret. Hints sit under each locked slot on the Shelf.

What's a Perfect Squish? And a Golden press?

Perfect Squish: close slowly and steadily, without easing off. Your card gets a quiet Perfect Squish mark. Slamming works too, but it never earns anything.

Golden: about 1 in 50 presses comes out Golden. It's purely cosmetic, every press has the same odds, and the odds are shown in Settings under About.

Is anything real supposed to go inside?

No. Everything in Squish is on screen. Please never close your phone on real objects.

Did I lose my collection?

Your collection lives only on your iPhone, so deleting the app deletes it. We can't restore it, because we never had it.

Do I need to buy anything later?

No. One price, no in-app purchases and no ads. New objects arrive as free updates.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so we can't issue refunds ourselves. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com, signed in with the Apple Account you bought Squish with.

Reporting a bug

The bug report form asks for:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings › General › About)
  • the Squish version (Squish Settings, under About)
  • the object you were pressing, and whether you were folding or dragging
  • what happened, and what you expected

A screen recording helps a lot. Privacy: Squish collects no data; see the Privacy Policy.
