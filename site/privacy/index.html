Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026 · Last updated 23 October 2026

The short version: Squish doesn't collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app makes no network requests of its own. Your collection stays on your phone. On the App Store this is declared as Data Not Collected.

What Squish collects

Nothing. Squish has no sign-up and no login. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code from anyone, and it doesn't ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts or location. It never reads your photo library: it only asks to add a picture or clip to Photos when you choose Save in the share sheet.

What stays on your device

Your pressed specimens, shelf, unlocks, achievements in progress and settings are saved only on your iPhone. We can't see them. If you delete the app, they're deleted too, and we can't restore them, because we never had them.

If a later version ever adds something like iCloud sync, it will use your own iCloud account, and we'll update this policy before that version is released.

Sharing

When you tap Share, Squish makes a short clip or image on your phone and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next is up to you. Nothing is sent to us.

Game Center (optional)

If you're signed in to Game Center, Squish reports the achievements you earn to Apple's Game Center. That is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't receive your Game Center profile or any other data from it. If you're not signed in, achievements you earn are kept on your iPhone until you are.

Crash reports

If you've chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports. We use them only to fix bugs. You can turn this off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Tracking

Squish doesn't track you across apps or websites, and it never asks for tracking permission.

Children

Squish collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics, trackers, embedded videos, external fonts or scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

If you contact us

Support requests go through GitHub Issues. Anything you post there is public and is handled by GitHub under its privacy statement, so please don't include personal details you don't want others to see. We only use what you send to reply to you and fix the problem. We never add you to a mailing list. If you posted something personal by mistake, say so in the issue and we'll remove it.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the dates at the top before the change takes effect in the app. The full history of this page is public in the project repository.

Contact

Squish, an independent app by GitHub user pravegm. Questions about privacy: open a support request.
