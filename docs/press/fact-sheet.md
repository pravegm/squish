# Squish: fact sheet

**Squish turns a folding phone into a hydraulic press.** A virtual object sits on the lower half of the iPhone Duo's inner screen. Close the phone slowly and the press follows the hinge degree by degree: the object strains, cracks and gives way, with sound and haptics in the same frame. Close it fully for a *ka-chunk*, reopen to find it flattened and filed as a pressed specimen. On every other iPhone you drag the ram down instead.

No embargo. Everything here is free to use now.

## Video in this folder

Real screen recordings of the app (Xcode's iPhone Duo and iPhone 18 Pro Max simulators), with the app's own sound, re-rendered from the same press. Nothing is staged or animated by hand. Each is H.264 with AAC stereo, so it plays everywhere and can be posted as it is.

| File | What it shows | Spec |
|---|---|---|
| `squish-duo-duck.mp4` | iPhone Duo, tabletop: a rubber duck squashes as the top half closes; the phone reopens to its specimen card, stamped PRESSED | 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, 9.5 s, with sound |
| `squish-glass.mp4` | Any iPhone (drag to press): a glass bottle creaks under the press, holds, then breaks | 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, 7 s, with sound |
| `squish-can.mp4` | Any iPhone: a soda can buckles ring by ring, then comes back as a specimen card | 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, ~9 s, with sound |
| `squish-glass-loop.gif` | The glass bottle breaking, looped, for live blogs and chat | 360 × 640, 3.4 s, silent |

On the narrow phone screens the clips sit on a blurred copy of themselves, so no part of the app is cropped. The App Store previews (886 × 1920, 19–26 s, with sound) are on the press-kit release.
