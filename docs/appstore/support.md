## Quick answers

**How do I press?**
- **On iPhone Duo:** open the phone fully on the inner screen, ideally lying flat on a table. Push the top half down **slowly**, and the press follows your hand. Close it fully for the *ka-chunk*, then open it again to see your specimen.
- **On any other iPhone:** drag down on the screen and the P-48 press follows your finger. It's the same press and the same objects. (There is no pinch gesture: `RootView.touchPress` is a drag.)
- **With VoiceOver** (when pressing by dragging): the press is one adjustable control. Swipe up to press down, swipe down to ease off.

**Is anything real supposed to go inside?**
No. Everything in Squish is on screen. Please never close your phone on real objects. Your inner screen will thank you.

**Can I play on iPhone Duo without folding?**
Yes. Open the Shelf, tap the cog, and turn on **Press without folding**. Every object, unlock and achievement works this way.

**How do I unlock the other objects?**
Just keep pressing. Six objects are open from the start, three more unlock through normal play (no purchases, ever), and one is a secret. Hints sit under each locked slot on the Shelf.

**What's a Perfect Squish?**
Close slowly and steadily, without stopping or reversing. Your card gets a quiet Perfect Squish mark. Slamming works too, but it never earns anything.

**What's a Golden press?**
About 1 in 50 presses comes out Golden. It's purely cosmetic, and the odds are shown in Settings under About.

**Too loud, too crunchy, or too much buzzing?**
In Settings (Shelf → cog), turn Sound off or choose the **Soft** sound style for a gentler mix, and set Haptics to Soft or Off. **Captions** shows big comic words like POP! and CRACK! for each moment, useful with the sound off.

**No sound?**
Squish respects the silent switch and mixes with your music (`.ambient` audio session). Flip the switch, turn the volume up, and check Sound is on in Settings. Haptics are set separately.

**Reduce Motion?**
Squish follows the system setting (Settings › Accessibility › Motion), and its own Settings screen shows whether it's on. Fragments slump and settle instead of flying.

**Game Center?**
Optional. Signed in, achievements are reported as you earn them; signed out, they're kept on the iPhone and sent once you sign in (Settings › Game Center).

**Did I lose my collection?**
Your collection lives only on your phone, so deleting the app deletes it. We can't restore it, because we never had it.

**Do I need to buy anything later?**
No. One price, no in-app purchases and no ads. New objects arrive as free updates.

**Family Sharing?**
Yes. Squish is available through Family Sharing.

**Refunds**
Purchases are handled by Apple. Request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Reporting a bug
Please include:
- your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About)
- the object you were pressing
- what happened vs. what you expected

A screen recording helps a lot.

**Contact:** [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/pravegm/squish/issues/new/choose) · Squish (GitHub `pravegm`)
Privacy: see our [Privacy Policy](https://pravegm.github.io/squish/privacy/). Squish collects no data.
