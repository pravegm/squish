# Squish: shoot guide (real hands, real iPhone Duo)

*For the founder. No video experience needed. Written 26 Oct 2026.*

**Why this matters.** A real hand closing a real Duo is the format Duo viewers reward. A 20-second Short of someone simply playing on a Duo reached 2.44M views from a 48.8k-subscriber channel (`BENCHMARK.md` B13). The simulator can't make this footage; only you can.

**Time:** about **90 minutes**, including 10 minutes of setup and 10 minutes unlocking objects.

**You need:**
- your iPhone Duo;
- a second phone as the camera;
- a tripod or phone clamp (or a stack of books);
- one or two household lamps;
- a sheet of white or cream card (about £2 from a craft shop) or a plain desk;
- a microfibre cloth;
- a sticky-note pad and a pen.

---

## ⚠️ The safety rule (read this first; it applies to every shot)

1. **Nothing real ever goes between the halves.** The phone always closes on nothing but its own screen. Every object in Squish is on the screen.
2. **No real versions of the app's objects anywhere in the room shot.** No bottle, rubber duck, egg, cake or can on the desk, not even in the background. Viewers must never get the idea.
3. **Always a gentle, normal close.** Take 3–6 seconds and let the magnets do the last bit. **Never slam, squeeze, press hard on the screen, or press near the hinge.** The inner screen is soft. "CrackGate" (reports of inner screens cracking near the hinge) is the top Duo story this week (`BENCHMARK.md`), and a press app must look careful.
4. **Secure the camera phone** so it can't fall onto the Duo.
5. When you clean the screen, wipe gently with a dry microfibre cloth. Don't push.

If a take breaks any of these, delete it. We never use it, not even as a blooper.

## 1. Prepare the Duo (10 minutes)

- **Charge** it above 60%. Take the **case off**. Wipe the screen gently.
- **Turn on Airplane Mode** (Control Centre). That stops calls and messages completely, and Squish needs no internet.
- **Turn on Do Not Disturb as well** (Control Centre › Focus › Do Not Disturb).
- **Hide notification previews:** Settings › Notifications › Show Previews › **Never**. Change it back afterwards.
- **Brightness:** drag it to **full**. Then switch off Settings › Accessibility › Display & Text Size › **Auto-Brightness**, so it doesn't dim mid-shot.
- **Switch off True Tone and Night Shift** (Settings › Display & Brightness) so colours stay the same from take to take.
- **Low Power Mode off.**
- **Keep it cool.** Shoot in 15-minute blocks and rest the phone between them. Squish turns effects down when the phone runs warm.
- **Add Screen Recording to Control Centre:**
  1. Open Control Centre and tap **+** (top left).
  2. Tap **Add a Control**.
  3. Choose **Screen Recording**.
- **Squish settings** (the gear key on the press console, or Menu › Settings):
  - Sound **on**, style **Workshop**;
  - Haptics **Full**;
  - **Play when phone is on Silent: on**;
  - Captions **off**;
  - Reduce Motion **off**;
  - Press without folding **off** (except for shot S10).
- **Volume:** about **80%**.

### Unlock every object before filming (about 10 minutes)

Just play for a bit:
1. **Soda Can:** press 3 different objects.
2. **Gummy Bear:** press 6 times in total.
3. **Glass Bottle:** do one **Perfect Squish** (close slowly and steadily for 2 seconds or more, without easing off).
4. **The steel ball:** press all 9 other objects.

**Golden** (1 in 50) is random. If one appears during filming, keep that take.

## 2. Prepare the camera phone

- **Settings › Camera › Record Video:**
  - **4K at 30 fps.**
  - **HDR Video off.** HDR often looks washed-out once uploaded to Instagram.
  - **If you see rolling bands** from LED lamps on playback (UK mains is 50 Hz): turn on **Show PAL Formats** and use **4K at 25 fps**.
- **Settings › Camera › Grid: on.**
- **Settings › Camera › Macro Control: on.** Then make sure the **macro (flower) icon is off** in the Camera app, so the lens doesn't jump.
- **Turn off location in photos:** Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Camera › **Never**. Your footage will then carry no home location.
- **Airplane Mode and Do Not Disturb** on this phone too.
- **In the Camera app:** Video mode, the **1×** lens (never 0.5×), phone held **vertically**.
  - Frame the shot, then **touch and hold on the Duo's lower screen** until **AE/AF LOCK** appears.
  - Drag the **sun icon down a little** (about one notch). The Duo screen should look bright and colourful, not glowing white, and the desk should still be visible.

## 3. Light it with household lamps

- **Turn the ceiling light off.** Overhead light makes a glare stripe on the screen and flattens everything.
- **Key light:** one desk or floor lamp to one side of the phone, slightly in front and above it, at about 45°.
  - **Soften it** by hanging a sheet of baking paper or a thin white pillowcase between the lamp and the phone.
  - **LED bulbs only**, and keep the fabric or paper at least 10 cm from the bulb.
- **Fill:** on the opposite side, prop up a white card, A3 paper or a white pillowcase on a box. It bounces light into the shadows. A second lamp, further away, also works.
- **Bulbs:** all the same colour. All warm or all cool; never mix them.
- **Daytime alternative:** a window with the curtain drawn as a diffuser, the phone side-on to the window. Ceiling light still off.
- **Test:** record 5 seconds and play it back on the camera phone at full brightness. The screen should be readable, the hand lit, with no bright stripe across the glass.

## 4. A clean background

- A plain light wood or off-white desk, or **a sheet of cream or white card** on the desk curving up the wall behind it. Squish's own backdrop is warm off-white (`#F3EEE6`), so cream matches.
- **Nothing else in the frame:**
  - no mugs, cables, papers, labels, other screens or brand logos;
  - no letters or anything with your name or address;
  - no windows showing the outside of your home.
- **Hands:**
  - clean nails;
  - plain sleeves with no logos, rolled back;
  - take off rings and watches (they reflect light and can scratch).
- Nobody else's face in frame unless they've happily agreed to be in a public post.

## 5. Framing for 9:16 (vertical)

- **Always shoot vertically.**
- **Keep the phone in the middle of the frame.** Fill about half to two-thirds of the frame's height with it.
- **Leave space:** about the **top 15%** and **bottom third** of the frame stay clear of anything important. Instagram's buttons and our captions go there.
- **The lower half of the Duo (the anvil, with the object on it) is the star.** Keep it near the centre. Your hand comes in from the side and **never covers the lower screen**.

**The four camera positions:**

| Position | Where the camera goes | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **A: three-quarter overhead** (the hero) | About 40 cm from the Duo and about 35 cm above the desk, tilted down about 45°, pointing at the lower screen | Most shots |
| **B: top-down** | Directly above the Duo, 35–40 cm up. Use an overhead phone stand, or a tripod arm reaching over. **Secure it**: nothing must be able to drop onto the Duo | Bubble wrap, the tabletop reveal, the every-iPhone shot |
| **C: low side** | At desk height, 30 cm away, looking **along the hinge** so you can see between the halves | The "gap is empty" proof shot |
| **D: close-up** | 15–20 cm from the **outer** screen | The "PRESS SEALED" card |

The Duo sits in **tabletop pose** unless the shot says otherwise: the lower half flat on the desk, the top half lifted, and your hand on the **top half's outer edge** pushing it down slowly.

## 6. Capturing the sound properly

**Plan A (best): screen recording and camera together.**
1. On the Duo, open Control Centre and **touch and hold** the Screen Recording button.
2. Make sure **Microphone is Off** (the label under the mic icon). With the mic off, the recording captures **clean in-app sound** with no room noise. Tap **Start Recording**. It starts after a 3-second countdown.
3. Start the camera phone recording.
4. Perform the shot.
5. Stop the camera, then stop the screen recording (the red indicator at the top, or Control Centre). Screen recordings save to Photos.

**How the producer lines them up:** the *ka-chunk* (the heavy sound when the phone shuts) is a sharp sound that's in both recordings. That's the sync point, so you don't need a clap.

**Plan B: if the screen recording stops or misbehaves when you close the phone.** That's fine: after each press, on the specimen card, tap **Share › Share Clip › Save Video**. Squish re-renders your exact press with full sound at 1080 × 1920. The camera clip gives us the hands; the share clip gives us the screen and sound.

**Either way:** a quiet room with no TV, music, washing machine or open window. The camera's own audio is a back-up.

## 7. How each take runs

1. Start the Duo screen recording (Plan A).
2. Start the camera.
3. Hold a **sticky note with the shot and take number** ("S4 T2") in front of the camera for one second, then take it away.
4. Wait 2 seconds, perform the shot, then hold still for 2 seconds.
5. Stop both recordings.
6. Do **3 takes of every shot.** Write the take you liked best on the sticky-note pad.

## 8. The shot list (12 shots, 3–8 seconds each)

*Concept codes refer to the asset brief in `PLAN.md` §0. Do them in this order (it minimises moving the camera).*

| # | Concept | Object | Camera | What the hand does | Length |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **S1** | C1, C2 alt | **Glass bottle** | A | Start with the phone open in tabletop pose and the bottle on the anvil. Close **very slowly** through the long creak; keep going steadily through the break; finish with a soft magnetic close | 6–8 s |
| **S2** | C1 (safety proof) | **Glass bottle** | **C** | The same slow close, filmed along the hinge, so viewers can see **the gap between the halves is empty** all the way down | 5–6 s |
| **S3** | C3 | **Bubble wrap** | B, plus Plan A screen recording (this one matters most) | The slowest close of the day, so the bubbles pop **one at a time** ("pop… pop… pop"). Then the final volley | 6–8 s |
| **S4** | **C2 hero** | **Egg** | A | **Begin recording with the phone already partly closed (about two-thirds open) and moving.** The top half is coming down from the first frame. A slow, steady close through the cracks, the yolk, then a soft magnetic close | 6–8 s |
| **S5** | C2 intro, Stories | Any (bubble wrap first) | B | **The tabletop reveal:** the phone starts closed and flat on the desk. The hand opens it into tabletop pose, and the object drops onto the anvil with its little bounce | 4–5 s |
| **S6** | C2 | — (straight after a press) | **D** | After the *ka-chunk*, the closed phone lies still. Film the outer screen showing "PRESS SEALED · 48 TONS · OPEN TO SEE". The hand stays out of frame | 3–4 s |
| **S7** | C1, C2 | Egg or glass bottle | A | **The reveal:** open the closed phone slowly. When the card appears, tap **SLO-MO** to replay the break, then let the red PRESSED stamp land | 6–8 s |
| **S8** | Post 5 | **Rubber duck** | A (camera a bit lower) | **Book pose:** hold the Duo in two hands like a book, opened vertically, and close it slowly. Hands at the outer edges, never on the screens | 5–6 s |
| **S9** | C6 (held) | **Steel ball** | A | A slow close; the gauge redlines and the press's **yellow ram** cracks. **Then open the phone fully and hold it there, intact,** for 2 seconds. That's the pull-back the edit needs | 6–8 s |
| **S10** | C4 | **Soda can** | B | **Every iPhone:** on a non-Duo iPhone if you have one, or on the Duo opened **flat** with Settings › **Press without folding** on. One finger drags the P-48 ram down slowly; the can buckles ring by ring | 5–6 s |
| **S11** | C2 insert (optional) | — | A or a wider shot | **The reaction:** after a satisfying press, a small, natural hands-only beat (fingers resting on the closed phone, a little tap). If someone happily agrees to be on camera, their real laugh works too. Only if it looks natural | 3 s |
| **S12** | C2 tail, C5, Stories | **Jelly, crayon, cake, soda can, gummy bear, rubber duck** | A, **unchanged** | **Montage source:** six presses in a row, one per object, same angle and same lighting, each a normal slow close. The editor cuts about 3 s from each | 3 s each (about 30 s total) |

**Bonus, no camera needed (10 minutes):**
- For each of the 10 objects, after the press: **Share › Share Clip › Save Video**, and **Share Image**.
- Make one **Golden** card if you get one. Never fake it.
- **Leave the steel ball's share clip in a separate folder** marked "SECRET". It isn't used until the founder decides (`PLAN.md` C6).

## 9. Sending the files to me

1. **Make one folder** with today's date, e.g. `SquishShoot-2026-10-27`, containing three sub-folders: `camera`, `screen`, `share-clips`. Add a note file `shot-log.txt` with your favourite take of each shot ("S4: take 2").
2. **Move the videos to your Mac with AirDrop:**
   - On each iPhone, open Photos, select the clips, then Share › AirDrop › your Mac.
   - **On an Intel Mac, AirDropped files land in your Downloads folder**: `/Users/<your Mac username>/Downloads`. In Finder: Go › Downloads, or press ⌥⌘L.
   - Drag them from Downloads into the right sub-folder.
3. **Don't trim, edit, compress or rename the clips.** Send the originals.
4. **Tell me the full path of the folder.**
   - **If I'm working on your Mac:** e.g. `/Users/<you>/Movies/SquishShoot-2026-10-27`.
   - **If I'm working on this Windows PC** (where the Squish repo is, `C:\Users\59527\Squish`): copy the folder to **`C:\Users\59527\SquishShoot\2026-10-27\`** with a USB stick or drive, or plug the iPhone into the PC and import with the Windows Photos app. Then send me that path.
5. **Keep the footage out of the Squish repo** (it's public) and out of shared work folders. I'll pick out and export only the finished cuts.

**Size:** 4K at 30 fps runs about 170 MB a minute (the figure the iPhone's Record Video setting shows) **[estimate]**, so expect 3–6 GB for the whole shoot. Make sure your Mac has the space before you start AirDropping.
