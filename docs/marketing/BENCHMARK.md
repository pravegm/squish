# Squish: content benchmark

*Snapshot taken Mon 26 Oct 2026, three days after iPhone Duo went on sale. It is the evidence base for `PLAN.md` and its asset brief. Every benchmark is cited there as **B1–B15**.*

## How this was gathered, and its limits

- **Method.** I read public pages only, with no logins and no downloads. For YouTube I read each video's public page data (title, length, views, likes, upload date, description, channel subscribers) on 26 Oct 2026. Other sources were App Store listings, websites, press coverage and published case studies.
- **I did not watch the videos frame by frame.** Where a hook is marked *(inferred)*, I worked it out from the title, the description, the length and the genre convention. Treat it as a strong guess, not an observation.
- **Instagram and TikTok numbers weren't reachable.**
  - Instagram and TikTok profiles sit behind a login wall for scripted reads.
  - The **Meta Ad Library** (`facebook.com/ads/library`) returned HTTP 403, and facebook.com is blocked for this browser by organisation policy.
  - **TikTok Creative Center** (`ads.tiktok.com`) is blocked the same way, and its page needs JavaScript to show any ads.
  - **So YouTube Shorts is the proxy.** The same satisfying and tech creators cross-post one vertical cut to Shorts, Reels and TikTok, so the formats carry over even though the numbers won't match exactly.
  - **Founder to-do (5 minutes, logged in on your own phone):** open the Meta Ad Library, set Country to UK, search "satisfying game", "hydraulic press" and "asmr game", and screenshot three ads that have run for more than 30 days. Ads that run a long time are usually the ones making money.
- **Views depend on channel size,** so the table shows views as a multiple of subscribers too (**v/s**). A high v/s means the platform pushed the clip to people who don't follow the channel. That is the result Squish needs from a brand-new account.
- **Like rate** = likes ÷ views.

## Synthesis

### 1. Do / don't for Squish content

| Do | Because | Don't | Because |
|---|---|---|---|
| Put the **object under load in frame 1** (no logo, no title card, no "hey guys") | B3–B9 all open on the material; the 10–23 s satisfying Shorts are the ones with 3–54M views | Open on the phone at rest, a logo or a talking head | B11: the explainer cuts (39–54 s) are the weakest Duo-app format |
| **Lead with the glass bottle**, then bubble wrap, egg and soda can | Glass is the top object in the niche (B4, B5: 16–18M) | Lead with the secret steel ball | It's a payoff, not an opener, and the press kit asks us to keep it secret (`docs/press/fact-sheet.md`). Hold it for week 3 |
| Keep cuts at **8–17 s** for satisfying clips and **15–20 s** for Duo hands-on | B5, B6, B9 (8–11 s); B4 (17 s); B13 Grixti (20 s) | Go past 25 s with a *single* object | The longer winners are multi-object compilations (B3, 47 s; B1 long-form) or device tours (AppleInsider, 118 s). A single press doesn't hold attention past about 20 s |
| Use the **app's own crunch, pop and creak; no music** | B6: the sound *is* the content; the bible §8 says music suppresses the ASMR response | Lay trending audio over ads | Ad music licences are restricted on Meta and TikTok, and it hides our best asset (see §4) |
| **Show real hands on a real Duo** for the Duo audience | B13: 2.44M views on a 48.8k-subscriber channel | Fake the hinge with an edit, or stage anything physical | B15a, plus DECISIONS: never anything real between the halves |
| **End on a frame that loops** (the duck back on the empty anvil) | B4–B9 are built to replay; replays and sends drive Reels distribution (secondary sources, see PLAN §1) | Put a long end card with a URL in the video | It breaks the loop; the link goes in the caption, bio or sticker |
| Say **"No ads. No in-app purchases."** in captions and ads | B14: reviews of crush games complain about ad farms | Imitate Hydraulic Press Channel's look, catchphrases or name | Brand rules (`research/06-market.md` §3) |
| Keep the **close gentle and slow**, and show the gap empty | CrackGate context; Guideline 1.4.5 | Slam, squeeze, or say "crush your phone" or "crack" about the *phone* | CrackGate comments and App Store safety rules |
| Use the **price joke in words, not on the video** | B10, B11: "world's most expensive toaster", "the best use of the $1999 iPhone Duo" | Burn "£2.49" into organic clips | Keeps the clips reusable (existing rule in `social-launch.md`). A paid-ad-only end card is the one exception |

### 2. Hook formulas, ranked by the evidence

1. **Object at the moment of strain, no words for 0.5 s, then a ≤6-word deadpan line.** *"it creaks. then it doesn't."* Evidence: B4, B5, B6, B7.
2. **The physical joke as a statement.** *"my £2,000 hydraulic press"*, with the fold already moving in frame 1. Evidence: B10, B11, B12, B13.
3. **Line-up, then "which one goes first?".** Evidence: B8, B15b (opinion-bait drives comments and serial posting).
4. **Single-material ASMR, sound-led, loopable, 8–10 s.** Evidence: B9, B7.
5. **The subversion (the object wins).** *"this one breaks the press"*. Evidence: B1 (HPC's best-loved videos are the objects that resist), B15a (a fail hook, but a true one). **Hold until week 3.**
6. **"No fold? drag it."**, the every-iPhone utility hook. Evidence: B10 leads its listing with "plays on any iPhone". It's a weaker hook but a much wider audience, so it's for ads to all iPhones, not organic.

### 3. Best length and structure

- **Satisfying cut (main organic format):** 9–13 s. Object under load (0–0.5) → strain with rising sound (0.5–3) → break (3–4) → SLO-MO replay of the break (4–6) → card stamp (6–8) → loop frame (8–9). Matches B4–B7.
- **Duo hands-on cut:** 13–18 s. The fold moving in frame 1 → slow close → break → magnetic close and *ka-chunk* → open → card. Matches B13 and B12.
- **Line-up:** 15 s. Four objects, about 3 s each, then the question. Matches B8.

### 4. Sound strategy

- **Default: in-app sound only.** That is the recorded foley, the synth, the *ka-chunk* and the reveal sting.
- **Name the audio when you post** ("Squish – glass bottle"), so other creators can reuse it. Reused audio sends viewers back to your clip.
- **Trending audio:** only on *organic* TikTok, under the app sound at no more than 10–15% volume, and never on ads.
  - Meta: Business accounts and ads are limited to licensed or Sound Collection music.
  - TikTok: ads must use the Commercial Music Library.
  - A copyrighted track is also the most common reason ads get muted or rejected.
  - Our crunch beats any track anyway (B6).
- **Captions for sound-off viewers:** a ≤6-word hook for about 1.5 s, then a clean frame. Optional on-screen words (POP!, CRACK!) can come from the app's own Captions setting.

### 5. When to show the phone, and when to show the screen

- **Satisfying audience (ads to every iPhone, Reels Explore):** screen first, phone second. Open on a tight crop of the lower screen so the object fills the frame (B4–B9). Then **pull back at about 3–4 s** to reveal that the "press" is a phone closing. The reveal is a second hook.
- **Duo audience (r/iPhoneDuo, Duo creators, the "Duo flex" ad):** the phone and hands from frame 1 (B13). The fold is what they came for.
- **Every-iPhone ad:** a screen recording of the P-48 with a finger visible. No foldable in frame, so nobody thinks they need a Duo.

### 6. Posting cadence

- **Founder benchmark:** satisfying channels post daily (Hydraulic Party, Sand Tagious and SplashAvalanche each upload many Shorts a week, from their channel pages).
- **Realistic for a solo founder:** **1 Reel a day in launch week, then 4 a week for weeks 2–4**, each cross-posted the same day to TikTok and Shorts. Plus **Stories daily in launch week**, then 3 a week. ALttL's creator wave came from a *series* (B15b), so consistency beats single big swings.

### 7. Money versus organic effort (net per sale: £1.76 in the UK, $2.12 in the US, see PLAN §2)

| Channel | Verdict | Numbers (estimates are marked) |
|---|---|---|
| Organic Reels, TikTok, Shorts | **All effort, £0** | B3 and B13 show 50–63× views-per-subscriber breakouts from small accounts; nothing here is cheaper per view |
| Creator seeding (promo codes) | **High effort, £0** | 100 codes per version (Apple). ALttL's biggest wave was unpaid creators (B15b) |
| Press, FoldHunt, Apple featuring | **Medium effort, £0** | Ridiculous Fishing and Toastie both reached the top paid charts on press and featuring (B10, B15b) |
| Apple Ads (search) | **£50 test** | The only channel whose estimated CPI (£1.40–£2.50) is near break-even (PLAN §2) |
| Meta (Instagram) ads | **£30 test, only behind a proven Reel** | Estimated CPI £8–£30 for a paid app from cold social traffic: loses money per sale; buys reach and learning |
| TikTok ads, YouTube ads, paid creators | **£0 in the first £100** | Ad-group minimums and creator fees exceed the whole budget *(estimate)* |
| Product Hunt | **Low effort, optional** | Low impact for consumer toys (`research/06-market.md` §Launch plan) |
